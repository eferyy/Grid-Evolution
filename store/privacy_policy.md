# Privacy Policy — Grid Evolution

_Last updated: 2026-02_

**Grid Evolution** ("the app", "we", "us") is a single-player offline puzzle game developed by **Dinzdp** and published at the Google Play package `com.dinzdp.gridevolution`.

## Data we collect
**None.** Grid Evolution does **not** collect, transmit, store on a server, or share any personal or non-personal data about you or your device.

## Data stored on your device
The app stores the following items **locally on your device only**, using standard application storage (WebView `localStorage`):
- Your high score, highest level reached, and score multiplier
- Saved game session (tiles, moves, power-ups) so you can resume
- Sound and haptics preferences
- Onboarding completion flag

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it completely. You can also clear it any time from the in-game Settings → "Reset Progress".

## Network
The app does not require and does not use an internet connection. No `INTERNET` permission is requested.

## Permissions
Grid Evolution requests **no runtime permissions**. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, media, storage, or any other sensor.

## Third-party services
None. No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting, no cloud sync.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from users, including children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be available at the same URL and included with app updates.
