## Full description

**Grid Evolution** is a refined, dark-themed take on the classic 2048 puzzle. Pastel colors, fluid animations, and soft haptic feedback create a calm mental-training experience.

✦ **Features**
• Smooth swipe controls with live move preview
• Growing board as you level up: 4×4, 5×5, 6×6
• Undo, Hammer, and Shuffle power-ups
• "Void" blocks — strategic obstacles that demand attention
• Hexa Evolution, Penta, and Quadruple combos
• Score multiplier and persistent high-score tracking
• Soft pastel theme, gentle sounds, minimal UI
• Session autosave — pick up where you left off

✦ **Why Grid Evolution?**
• **Fully offline** — no internet required
• **Zero permissions** — no camera, no location, no recording
• **No ads, no in-app purchases** — just the game
• **No personal data collected** — your scores stay on your device

Let your mind evolve. Relax, focus, and reach the next tier with Grid Evolution.
